Inspiring Healthy Habits, Lifelong Results.

Hypertension is the medical term for high blood pressure- when the force of blood pushing against your arteries is too high. It is also known as “the silent killer”, as it often has no symptoms, but over time it can increase the risk of heart attack, stroke, kidney disease and other complications.

What is Hypertension treatment adherence?

Hypertension treatment adherence means how well a person follows their blood pressure treatment plan, especially taking medicines as prescribed and keeping up with lifestyle changes and follow-up visits

© Pexels - @RDNE

Why is Medication Adherence Important?

Taking blood pressure medication exactly as prescribed is a major factor in controlling blood pressure and preventing complications like heart attack, stroke and death. It helps avoid worsening the disease, leading to a healthier life, and longer benefits. Consistent adherence to treatment must be maintained over time and changes to treatment must be discussed with your healthcare provider.

How to Improve Medication Adherence

Alerts

Set phone alarms to make taking medication consistent.

Check-ups

Maintain routine follow up visits to reduce missed doses.

Dosage

Use “once daily” medicines to simplify dosing.

Aided

Include family support and nurse-led coaching when possible.
