Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about managing hypertension, monitoring your blood pressure, and staying on track with your medication.

Can hypertension be prevented?

Yes. A healthy diet, regular exercise, maintaining a healthy weight, and avoiding smoking can help significantly reduce the risk.

Why is blood pressure monitoring important?

Regular checks can help detect hypertension early and help determine if your current lifestyle changes and treatments are working effectively.

Why is medication adherence so important?

Missing doses can reduce treatment effectiveness and lead to disease progression, or cause severe heart complications that could have otherwise been avoided.

What should I do if I miss a dose?

It depends on the medication. If you miss a single dose, you may take it immediately unless it is very close to the next dose. If you miss multiple doses, you must contact your doctor or pharmacist for guidance.

Never take multiple doses to catch up, as it may cause a severe and dangerous drop in your blood pressure.

Are side effects a reason to stop taking my medication?

No. You must consult your doctor first. Some side effects are manageable or strictly temporary, and stopping your medication immediately may be harmful to your health.

What if I cannot afford my medication?

If your medication is too expensive, you may ask your doctor about more affordable generic options or safe alternative treatments.

How long does it take to see results in my treatment?

The time may vary depending on the medication; it may take anywhere between a few hours to a few weeks to see changes in your blood pressure readings. You should consult your doctor or pharmacist to give you an accurate timeline for your specific treatment plan.
